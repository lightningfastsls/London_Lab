## Reasoning

The useful unit is a solved image family, not visual inspection volume. The batch was chosen to cover four different provenance patterns:

- prose-only handoff provenance with source data but missing generator (`00_raw_waveform.png`)
- committed generator with a known slide-content mismatch (`training_set_composition.png`)
- copied analysis result with strong script/source provenance (`umap_by_type.png`)
- sampled spectrogram gallery with source WAV and helper renderer (`Flat/01_*.png`)

This also exposed that byte-identical reproduction and content reproduction are different standards. For presentation rebuilds, content reproduction may be enough; for audit-grade provenance, record whether byte identity was actually tested.
