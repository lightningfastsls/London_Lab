VAE vs VQ-VAE: does the missing quantizer explain the failed shape clustering?

Assessment for Mickey's hypothesis + analysis-stack review · 2026-07-03 · grounded in code, artifacts, and the project's own falsification record

The two-sentence answer

Mickey is factually right that we built a plain VAE, not a VQ-VAE — verified in the code. But the evidence we already gathered predicts a VQ-VAE would not produce the visible shape classes he's after, because the failure was diagnosed to the learned 2-D pixel objective and to the fact that USV shape is a continuum with no discrete natural kinds — neither of which a quantizer fixes.

Part 1 — The hypothesis, checked against the code

Q: Did we accidentally build a VQ-VAE? No — plain β-VAE

The autoencoder whose latent space failed to separate shapes is a textbook continuous-latent VAE. There is no codebook, no quantization, no commitment loss anywhere in it:

  • Model: ImageVAE — 32-dimensional continuous Gaussian latent, 4-layer conv encoder/decoder. Defined in scripts/train_contour_vae_v2.py:117-289 (production trainer), mirrored on branch worktree-vae-pytorch-pivot:usv_language/models/image_vae.py.
  • Loss: ELBO = BCE reconstruction + KL divergence, β=1.0 (standard ELBO, not even a β-VAE weighting) — train_contour_vae_v2.py:269-289.
  • Bottleneck: reparameterization trick z = mu + eps*std. No nn.Embedding codebook, no argmin over prototypes, no straight-through estimator.
  • Input: contour-masked spectrogram patches, band-cropped and padded to 128×128.
  • Two sibling models (a DeepSqueak-faithful VAE and a 1-D registered-ridge VAE) are also plain VAEs.
Surprise finding: a real, fully-implemented VQ-VAE already exists in the repo — usv_language/src/model/quantizer.py + vqvae.py (512-entry codebook, EMA updates, straight-through estimator, dead-code reset). But it belongs to a separate "USV language / grammar" project (encoder → VQ → causal transformer), it was never trained (zero .pt weights on disk), and it was never pointed at the shape-clustering task. So Mickey's direction is partly pre-built — it just needs repurposing and training.

Mickey's mechanism is sound — but it's not the bottleneck that broke

His reasoning is legitimate: vector quantization snaps every encoder output to the nearest of K codebook vectors, so the model must explain all inputs with a finite alphabet of prototypes. That is a stronger structural prior than a Gaussian latent. The problem is that our diagnosis located the failure upstream of the bottleneck, and in the geometry of the data itself.

Four reasons the evidence predicts a VQ-VAE won't deliver separable classes

1. The failure is the learned 2-D pixel objective, not the continuity of the latent. A linear probe on the frozen contrastive encoder scored chevron-vs-not at balanced accuracy 0.517 versus 0.501 for a random-initialized network of the same architecture — a +0.001 gap. The encoder learned essentially nothing about shape a random projection didn't already have. A codebook bolted onto that same encoder inherits the same blindness. Six of six 2-D image-objective variants were falsified:

Human-anchored cross-check: the trained 32-D latent scored NMI 0.287 vs a no-learning column-mean-pixel-averaging baseline of 0.245 (+0.042, below the 0.10 gate). The latent adds almost nothing over averaging pixels down each time column.

2. There are no discrete natural kinds for a codebook to recover. Independent methods converge on USV shape being a filled continuum, not separable clusters:

  • WS-C topology (elastic-FPCA coords, 66k+ calls): one single connected component across all seeds, intrinsic dimension ≈5, zero persistent loops (H₁=0), no detached pocket. A genuinely high-D blob, not a set of islands.
  • WS-D archetypes: R² rises smoothly across the whole K-sweep with no elbow → "no privileged K — continuum confirmed." Every cohort spreads across the whole simplex.
  • Our HDBSCAN (7,864 calls): 96.6% collapse into one manifold. Goffinet 2021 (different method/data): VAE+GMM supported only k≤2 for mice. Cross-method agreement.
A quantizer imposes K reference points on a continuum. That gives a useful coarse index — like naming colors on a spectrum — but it cannot manufacture separable classes the geometry does not contain. This is already recorded in the project's own methodology tension file, which resolved that a codebook here is "pragmatic discretization of a continuum, not a categorical claim."

3. Discretization empirically loses information on this kind of data. The vault already logged Sarkar & Magimai-Doss 2025 on marmosets: post-hoc VQ scored 35% vs 49% UAR against continuous representations — "individual signatures live in fine-grained continuous variation that discrete codes cannot capture." Bioacoustic VQ is also failure-prone (Gumbel-softmax codebook collapse; no published end-to-end VQ-VAE on animal vocalizations yet).

4. The methods that actually won are the opposite of a discrete bottleneck — they're continuous, and their lever is warp-alignment, not representation learning:

The honest steelman (the one variant nobody ran). The falsification campaign only tested learned encoders on 2-D pixel substrates. Nobody trained a VQ-VAE on the correct substrate — the 1-D registered ridge or the elastic-FPCA coordinates. That is the single version of Mickey's idea we have not directly killed. But the signal against it is already strong: a plain continuous 1-D VAE on registered ridges (η² 0.497) already lost to doing nothing but registration (0.577). That says the win comes from removing pitch/position/duration, not from the bottleneck — so a discrete version on the same substrate would most likely also lose, while adding codebook-collapse risk. Low expected ROI, but it is the intellectually clean way to close the loop if Mickey wants a definitive test.

The distinction that resolves the disagreement: two different goals

GoalWill a VQ-VAE help?What to do
A. "Find the true classes of USVs" (Mickey's stated aim) No The classes don't exist as discrete kinds. The continuum is the finding — and it's a publishable scientific result, not a modeling failure. Report it as such.
B. "Get a discrete alphabet to run grammar / sequence analysis" Legitimate, different use A codebook as a coarse index is reasonable here. We already have a soft-DTW K=20 alphabet for this, and the untrained usv_language VQ-VAE could be trained if a learned alphabet is wanted. This is not "finding classes"; it's tiling a continuum for downstream MI/n-gram analysis.

My read: Mickey may be conflating A and B. The quantizer he's picturing solves B, not A — and B is already served.

Part 2 — The pipeline, and what to change to leave a good tool

The headline is not the science — it's the packaging. The science is settled and honestly documented: shape is a continuum, warp-alignment (soft-DTW) is the one real lever, the VAE family is correctly closed, and the current production representation is a hybrid (soft-DTW K=20 alphabet + elastic-FPCA coordinate system). What's missing is engineering. The detection side ships as a PyQt6 app; the analysis side is ~40 experiment scripts reading artifacts that live on the rig and in disposable job-scratch. That's the gap between "a research program" and "an analysis tool the lab can use without you."

Pipeline map (current production path)

  1. Detections → patches: per-call adaptive focus-STFT ridge (deepsqueak_focus_stft.py) → window to 234-bin patches (window_calls_to_patches.py) → contour-mask, raw-power (N,257,234) (mass_apply_contour_mask.py).
  2. Registered ridge (the shared substrate): Viterbi ridge-track → subtract mean pitch → resample active span to 50 points → true_registered_ridges.npz (67,337 ridges). Built by rig_R2_shape_alphabet.py — which currently lives under archive/cleaning_legacy/stack3/.
  3. Representation (hybrid): soft-DTW K=20 alphabet (models/shape_kmeans/k20_softdtw.joblib) as the accuracy metric + elastic-FPCA scores (models/shape_fpca/elastic_fpca_scores.parquet, 67,337 × {5 amp + 3 phase}) as the O(N) coordinate system.
  4. Downstream: continuum map (Track D), manifold topology (WS-C), archetypes (WS-D), grammar/transfer-entropy (WS-B, pure-numpy KSG), confound-robust cohort comparison (WS-E). Biology payoff (WS-F) is blocked on the LMT behavioral .sqlite.

Ranked weak points (what actually needs changing)

1Rescue & version the substrate and production modelsCritical

The two production model files exist on the box but are gitignored and untracked (.gitignore:107,110,112). The foundational true_registered_ridges.npz exists only in ephemeral job-scratch (/home/shachar/.claude/jobs/{57976676,b619c2bb}/tmp/shape_data/) — gone the moment those jobs are cleaned up. The build scripts live under the folder labeled "dead" (archive/cleaning_legacy/stack3/). A clean checkout cannot reproduce the pipeline today. Fix: promote ridges + both models into tracked artifacts (or DVC/lfs), un-archive the build code into a supported location, and add a one-command rebuild_shape_pipeline.sh. This is urgent and low-risk.

2Wrap the read-path into one tool (CLI or a PyQt tab)High

90% of real use is a parquet lookup, not model inference: given a call, show its letter + FPCA coordinates, render its registered ridge, show nearest neighbors and per-cohort stats. There is currently zero shape/repertoire surface in app/. This is what makes it "an analysis tool" for the next person rather than a pile of scripts.

3Unify the downstream lineages onto one joinHigh

Four disjoint substrates feed downstream (elastic-FPCA scores, discrete CNN labels, VQ-VAE codes, benchmark JSONs) with no cross-talk; "grammar" is computed twice on different substrates with no reconciliation. The FPCA↔detection join is subtle ((wav_stem, call_id−1) == (wav_stem, det_index); the tempting id column gives silently-wrong metadata) and is implemented twice with different tie-breaks. Consolidate to a single load_merged_fpca entry point.

4Close the evaluation-hygiene gapsMedium

Chevron label starvation (only 4 true Chevron; the ≥20 gate is unmet); scorecards unconditionally print "wild UNLABELED" even on wild-only label sets (now false across 4 cohorts); elastic-FPCA wasn't scored in the 5970 wild top-up; the grammar report labels its surrogate "bout-wise circular-shift" but the code does a global/pooled shift. These block a clean read of the Phase-3 ship/keep decision.

5Write the honest scientific verdict as the headline deliverableMedium

"USV shape is a graded continuum; warp-alignment is the one real lever; discrete/VAE approaches were exhaustively falsified" is the durable intellectual contribution of this work. Framed correctly, the continuum finding is a result, not a null. It should be the abstract of the analysis tool's documentation, not buried in handoffs.

My recommendation

Don't spend your remaining time building a VQ-VAE to find shape classes. The evidence predicts it won't produce separable classes (wrong bottleneck, wrong substrate, no discrete kinds to recover), and you'd be re-fighting a battle the project already won with the continuum finding. If Mickey wants the loop definitively closed, the one clean experiment is a VQ-VAE (or any learned encoder) on the registered-ridge / FPCA substrate rather than 2-D pixels — cheap to run, high scientific value as a final falsification, low probability of changing the verdict.

Do spend the time turning the settled science into a durable, reproducible analysis tool — recommendations 1–3 above are what convert this from "your research program" into "the lab's repertoire tool." Start with #1: it's urgent (the substrate is in disposable scratch), reversible, and needs no new modeling.
