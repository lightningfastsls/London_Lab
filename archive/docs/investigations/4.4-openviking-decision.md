## Decision

**OpenViking deferred — qmd retrieval is sufficient with query construction fixes.** The primary finding from 4.3 is that qmd's BM25 search performs well (90% P@1) when queries are properly constructed with 3-5 domain keywords. The "No strong matches" failure in session-orient was caused by a broken qmd shim on Windows, not retrieval quality. With the shim fix (4.1) and query construction improvements, qmd meets current needs. Vec search instability (Finding 1, 6) reduces the effective search modality to lex-only, but lex alone is sufficient for the current corpus size (523 notes). Revisit if corpus exceeds ~2000 notes or if description-based discovery becomes critical.
