Verdict

Navigable continuum — not discrete kinds, not a structureless blob.

All four shape families land at 2-D-map kNN purity whose 95% CI clears their base rate, on both the incumbent (Euclidean) and the elastic (soft-DTW) maps — so families are spatially organised, you can point to a "jump zone" vs a "flat zone". But the corpus is a single connected manifold whose mean-ridge thumbnails morph smoothly (chevron/valley at top → flat/ramps → sharp steps/jumps at bottom), and the K=20 soft-DTW letters tile it into contiguous territories. So this is a navigable continuum with the hard letters as a coarse index, not natural kinds carved at the joints. That reconciles why hard-letter clustering plateaued and elastic only won jump in the Phase-3 high-D kNN test.

Key finding — the elastic metric lifts the whole map, most where high-D tied. On the 2-D map, soft-DTW separates every family better than Euclidean, and the largest gains are on exactly the families that tied in the Phase-3 high-D test (complex +0.087, flat +0.046, chevron +0.045), while jump — which already won in high-D — gains least (+0.014). Viewed as a navigable map rather than as hard clusters, the elastic metric's value is broader than the Phase-3 jump-only result suggested.

What this means for the shape thread

  • The honest representation is the map, not the alphabet. Adopt the 2-D shape-map (soft-DTW preferred — it zones families best) as the repertoire representation; keep the K=20 letters as a coarse index/label into it, documented as "coarse tiling of a continuum", not discrete syllables.
  • Report shape as continuous axes / soft membership (position on the map, distance to family centroids) for the grammar & repertoire analyses, rather than forcing hard letters — consistent with the lower shape-vs-latent transition MI already on record.
  • The elastic win generalises beyond jump when framed as a map. This strengthens (does not overturn) the Phase-3 "keep registration as the production hard alphabet for now" call: the elastic metric's real payoff is a better continuous representation, not better hard clusters.
  • Caveats: complex/chevron N are small (67/44) and base rates are inflated by jump-heavy labeling; Map-B background is a 2k subsample; 2-D purity is descriptive, not a held-out gate. The falsified learned-encoder/VAE family stays closed.
