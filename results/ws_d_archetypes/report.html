Epistemic frame (WS-C). The shape space is one filled ~5-D blob — single connected component, intrinsic dim ≈5, zero persistent H1, no detached pocket. There are no natural kinds. Archetypes here are a resolution knob over a continuum, not cluster centres. The deliverable is robustness across K, not 'we found N types'. A feature appearing at only one K is an artifact.

5. Gate D — verdict

No privileged K — continuum confirmed. Explained variance has no elbow and resample stability decays as K grows; no value of K is singled out. This is the WS-C-consistent finding: the shape space is a filled blob, so archetypes are a resolution knob, not discrete kinds.
What IS stable. The archetype extremes that reappear at every K are the global continuum axes — primarily the amp_pc1 pitch/duration-amplitude axis (a high-|amp1| pole vs a near-centroid pole) and the leading phase (warp/timing) axis. Cohorts differ in where on the simplex they sit, but every cohort spreads across the whole simplex (no cohort owns an archetype). Cross-cohort simplex differences within the WILD stratum sit at the cage-confounded noise floor; the LAB cohort's position is the only one with a large, well-resolved n.
