Parameters (full transparency)

  • Shape coords = elastic-FPCA amp_pc1, amp_pc2 (≤2 FPCA dims/conditional, KSG bias budget)
  • pitch = principal_freq_hz; duration = call_length_s; timing = within-bout silent gap (begin[t+1]−end[t]). mean_power_db / tonality EXCLUDED (cage artifacts).
  • k ∈ {4, 6}; bout threshold ∈ {0.25 s (Stream-5 MI plateau), 0.6 s (corpus_facts 3× median IOI)}
  • TE = I(target_future ; source_past | target_past[, cond_past]); lag = 1 (single-step); cond appended to conditioning block
  • Bias floor = mean of 50 within-bout source SHUFFLES (destroys autocorr+coupling)
  • Surrogate = 200 bout-wise CIRCULAR shifts (preserves marginal autocorr, destroys cross-coupling); one-sided p=(#≥obs+1)/(N+1)
  • seed = 20260604; underpowered flag if < 200 within-bout pairs
  • Embedding NEVER crosses a bout boundary or a wav_stem boundary.

Verdict

[5970] After conditioning on pitch+duration and against bout-matched circular surrogates, shape→shape TE exceeds the autocorrelation null (TE−floor=0.0490 nats, surrogate p=0.005, z=5.67); timing→timing | shape TE survives (TE−floor=0.0846 nats, surrogate p=0.005, z=9.92). The continuum's grammar lives in both shape-order AND timing (comparable residual effect sizes).

[lab_131204] After conditioning on pitch+duration and against bout-matched circular surrogates, shape→shape TE exceeds the autocorrelation null (TE−floor=0.0139 nats, surrogate p=0.015, z=2.61); timing→timing | shape TE survives (TE−floor=0.1122 nats, surrogate p=0.005, z=22.84). The continuum's grammar lives in predominantly timing — both clear the surrogate null, but residual timing predictability is 8.1× the residual shape predictability (a GRADED dissociation, not a clean collapse).

3452 and 9252 are reported in the table but EXCLUDED from the verdict (within-bout pairs < 200; underpowered for conditional TE). 9252 is a confidently-wrong-CNN cohort — its shapes carry extra uncertainty.

Framing (Perrodin, Verzat & Bendor 2023, eLife 12:e86464). Female mice track temporal regularity (rhythm) and are invariant to syllable order and to individual-syllable spectro-temporal structure in a courtship-approach assay — but the order-scramble manipulation PRESERVED inter-syllable intervals, so the honest claim is "when rhythm is held constant, order/shape are not necessary for female approach", scoped to that behavioral assay. It is NOT a general claim that shape carries zero information. A shape-TE collapse here is CONSISTENT WITH (not proof of) that result, and must be reconciled with Chabout 2015 (4-symbol jump-code = pitch/duration/timing by another name) and Hertz 2020 (London lab, parsed by inter-syllable interval). This is a generative-structure (sequence-grammar) analysis, distinct from the behavioral-perception assay.
