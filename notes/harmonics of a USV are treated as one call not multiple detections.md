# Harmonics of a USV are treated as one call not multiple detections

When a USV produces harmonics — for example, a 45 kHz fundamental with a copy at 90 kHz — these are treated as one call, not two separate detections. This is a labeling convention that reflects the acoustic reality: the harmonic is generated by the same vocalization event and is not an independent signal. This convention matters for detection counting (avoids inflation) and for how the CNN learns to interpret multi-band energy patterns within the 20-120 kHz detection range. The [[frequency resolution of 586 Hz per bin suffices to distinguish USV subtypes in the 20-120 kHz range]], which is fine enough to resolve the fundamental-harmonic relationship.
