# USVs are one component of a multimodal courtship behavior suite including mounting approach and movement

In the recording context (male-female pairs), USVs function as one component of a multimodal courtship behavior suite that also includes mounting, approach, following, and other movements. This framing is essential: USVs are not isolated acoustic signals but are embedded in a behavioral sequence where vocal, locomotor, and tactile behaviors co-occur and potentially coordinate. This is why the project uses the [[Live Mouse Tracker from Institut Pasteur synchronizes vocalization recordings with social behavior events]] for temporal synchronization — enabling analysis of how USV types relate to concurrent and subsequent behavioral events. The [[combined cross-modal evidence from USV repertoire and MiceCraft movement data builds a stronger case for courtship degradation]] strategy leverages this multimodal nature.
