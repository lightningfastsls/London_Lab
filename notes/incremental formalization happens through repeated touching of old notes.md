# incremental formalization happens through repeated touching of old notes

Incremental formalization is the process by which informal, rough knowledge artifacts gradually become more precise through repeated interaction. In a knowledge graph, each time a note is encountered during purposeful traversal, the reader notices imprecisions — a vague description, a missing link, an overly broad claim.

Since [[traversal simultaneously improves the traverser and the graph creating a co-evolutionary loop]], these micro-edits accumulate into significant quality improvements without dedicated maintenance sessions. The formalization is incremental because no single touch fully formalizes a note; it happens gradually through dozens of encounters across sessions. This is why vault health improves as a side effect of purposeful work, and why dedicated "cleanup sprints" are less effective than regular traversal through active use.
