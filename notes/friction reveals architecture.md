# friction reveals architecture

Friction during vault operations — difficulty finding related notes, clunky traversal paths, repeated failure to surface relevant knowledge — is not noise to be ignored. It is diagnostic signal about the graph's structural properties. If a note is hard to find, its description may be optimized for humans rather than retrieval. If traversal feels disconnected, key bridging notes may be missing. If the same information keeps being re-derived, the derivation hasn't been captured as a note.

Since [[traversal simultaneously improves the traverser and the graph creating a co-evolutionary loop]], persistent friction signals that the co-evolutionary loop has stalled. The vault's observation system captures these friction signals in ops/observations/ so they accumulate until patterns emerge. Friction is not a failure of the user or the content — it is the graph telling you where its architecture needs work.
